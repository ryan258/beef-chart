# Content, functionality, documentation, and consumer/professional experience

## What currently exists, and how trustworthy is its content?

### Takeaway
The project is an attractive working beef atlas and frozen mixed-source retail comparison, with 24 cut records and useful Argentine/U.S. vocabulary. The greatest immediate opportunity is to make the atlas, source evidence, editorial terminology, and accessibility agree; adding more countries or charts first would amplify unresolved trust problems.

### Cited Findings
- Current frontend is one zero-build HTML file with inline CSS, embedded catalog and vanilla JavaScript/D3 logic, overridden by `assets/bauhaus.css`. It renders 20 mapped Argentine retail cuts, 4 specialty entries and 9 U.S. primals. Prices are fetched once from JSON, not streamed; there is no live in-browser provider query. — [catalog](../../index.html#L1133), [extra cuts](../../index.html#L1168), [price fetch](../../index.html#L1841), [architecture](../../docs/architecture.md).
- Mapped catalog includes cogote, aguja, paleta, pecho, brazuelo, bife ancho, asado de tira, matambre, bife de chorizo, vacío, tapa de cuadril, cuadril, colita, bola de lomo, nalga, cuadrada, garrón, lomo, entraña, peceto. Specialty bife de costilla is correctly distinct from boneless strip and T-bone; mollejas includes an organ-verification warning. — [index.html lines1133–1172](../../index.html#L1133).
- Current browser displays snapshot2026-10-07; BLS and INDEC August2026; USDA October2; Carrefour October7. Its bottom note explicitly warns that sources differ in date/method and estimates are illustrative. It also calls FX2026-08-31 “daily latest” for October7 Carrefour prices. The wording means newest returned record, not proof of fresh current FX; dates must be represented beside the comparison, not buried at page bottom. — [index.html lines1831–1838](../../index.html#L1831), [FX data lines386–395](../../data/prices.json#L386).
- Specialty cards currently show mollejas USD0.55/lb, kidney USD0.08/lb, tongue USD0.50/lb. All three underlying records are `out_of_stock`; only their opened modals expose that status. This is a serious representation problem even without proving whether the underlying price is wrong. USD conversions follow the frozen record values, but these are unavailable catalog prices, not purchasable offers. — [data/prices.json lines277–361](../../data/prices.json#L277), [card priceLine lines1193–1197](../../index.html#L1193), [modal status lines1574–1576](../../index.html#L1574).
- Bife de costilla correctly renders “Not available” and has no price entry. Missing data is not borrowed from another cut. — [specialty cards lines1095–1104](../../index.html#L1095), [price lookup lines1189–1197](../../index.html#L1189).
- Sankey flows total **104**, while wholesale primal shares total **100**. Each Sankey flow tooltip labels its value “% of carcass”; the extra4 Other-primals link causes an impossible whole-carcass sum. “Illustrative” does not resolve arithmetic contradiction. — [wholesale shares lines1156–1165](../../index.html#L1156), [Sankey links lines1364–1378](../../index.html#L1364), [percentage tooltip line1400](../../index.html#L1400). Runtime reduction of bound link data also returned104.
- Treemap represents hard-coded retail **value** shares summing100 (Rib24, Loin22, Chuck20, Round16, Brisket7, Plate5.5, Flank3.5, Other2), with no calculation from carcass weights or observed prices. README and architecture instead call it a “Primal Yield Treemap” and describe weight/yield. Its Other node opens the first shank cut, not an Other explanation. — [treemap lines1495–1523](../../index.html#L1495), [README line19](../../README.md#L19), [architecture line66](../../docs/architecture.md#L66).
- Sensory radar tenderness and marbling are unsourced cut-level constants; flavor, speed, crust and gelatin depend solely on broad `how` category (all grill cuts share9,8.5,9.5,2 on those axes). This looks measured but is an editorial heuristic; no preparation, grade, aging, animal, sample or panel methodology is supplied. — [cut constants lines1134–1153](../../index.html#L1134), [radar rules lines1653–1659](../../index.html#L1653).
- **Direct documented content contradiction:** live catalog calls entraña “Outside skirt”, while cuts-guide calls entraña fina “inside skirt”. Matambre is “Rose meat / Flank flap” in UI but “Rose Meat / Fly Shaker” and cutaneous trunci in guide. Those mappings require professional anatomical review; do not choose an answer from wording alone. Vacío/Flank/Bavette and aguja/chuck roll/chuck roast likewise need qualifiers rather than simple interchangeability. — [index lines1141,1143,1152](../../index.html#L1141), [cuts-guide lines62–70,85–87](../../docs/cuts-guide.md#L62).
- Guide contains prescriptive grilling timing (entraña8–10min, vacío40–60min), medium-rare-to-medium wording for strip, offal preparations and cultural universal “invariably”, without references or safety distinctions. The app supplies only grill/braise/roast plus generic Google recipe-search links called “Authentic Recipes”. A search result is not curated provenance or a verified recipe. — [guide lines40–115](../../docs/cuts-guide.md#L40), [modal lines1620–1638](../../index.html#L1620).
- USDA AMS describes IMPS as voluntary consensus meat product specifications used by institutional/food-service purchasers, provides Fresh Beef100 and Variety Meats700, and links a Spanish Fresh Beef100 version. This offers a primary evidence backbone for a professional bilingual cut dictionary; do not conflate IMPS with inspection/grading or assume unrestricted third-party Meat Buyers Guide rights. — [USDA IMPS](https://www.ams.usda.gov/grades-standards/imps).
- USDA FSIS indexed guidance recommends thermometer-verified whole beef steaks/roasts145°F/62.8°C plus3min rest, ground beef160°F/71.1°C, and organ/variety meats such as kidney/tongue160°F/71.1°C. The source page search returned this text, but direct fetch returned403, so these are authoritative indexed evidence rather than a fresh full-page retrieval. — [Beef From Farm to Table](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/meat-catfish/beef-farm-table), [safe temperature chart](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/safe-temperature-chart). Keep proposed consumer guidance separate from jurisdiction-specific professional kitchen procedures.
- README claims real-time/live cross-border intelligence, complete keyboard navigation and mobile down to320px. Roadmap marks WCAG AA contrast and compliant modal complete. These go beyond observed functionality. Docs say search instantaneously filters, but actual search only opens first name/equivalent/id match on Enter/button. Gallery is static six cards; “carousel” buttons open a modal for the next/previous list entry. About is a promotional card linking back to explorer, not methodology/about content. — [README lines3–32](../../README.md#L3), [roadmap](../../roadmap.md), [architecture lines44–76](../../docs/architecture.md#L44), [search/actions lines1730–1777](../../index.html#L1730), [About lines1083–1091](../../index.html#L1083).
- README recommends double-clicking HTML despite local JSON fetch and recommends `python3 -m http.server 8000`/`npx serve .` from repository root. A browser file-origin fetch can fail; root serving exposes repository contents and the Python default bind is broader than loopback. The review used a copied allowlist containing only index/assets/data bound to127.0.0.1. — [README lines90–102](../../README.md#L90).
- Assets include six cut WebPs, a steer portrait, pasture fence and unused vintage barn. No LICENSE/attribution/manifest filename was found in the bounded inventory; README asserts MIT, but asset source/generation permission is not independently established. The market Ground-chuck alt text reuses the Chuck Roast gallery photograph. Images have alt text, but naming and provenance should be audited, and gallery image names duplicate card accessible names. — [README lines56–61,126–128](../../README.md#L56), [market image line942](../../index.html#L942), [gallery lines1050–1078](../../index.html#L1050).

### Inferences
- The strongest distinct value proposition is an evidence-backed cross-cultural whole-animal beef reference with transparent price context and useful cooking/buying decisions. It has a clear editorial/design identity worth preserving.
- Professional credibility depends on scoped cut specifications, trim/bone/grade/region differences, measured/illustrative data boundaries and source provenance. More feed integrations alone will not supply those.
- Describing fixed sensory numbers as heuristics or replacing radar with qualified practical traits would be more useful than extending speculative precision.

### Gaps
- No professional butcher/anatomist reviewed mappings in this run. All regional cut equivalences remain subject to qualified editorial review.
- No live retailer buying/stock/price verification or fresh provider refresh occurred; this review does not establish current real-world prices.
- No image provenance/license evidence was located; absence of a manifest does not establish infringement.
- GitNexus graph-first query was attempted through project wrapper, remained silent and was canceled; parent code reviewer also observed runtime unavailability. No graph freshness/reindex or full suite was performed. Source/DOM inspection is explicitly the fallback evidence.

## How does the current experience behave on desktop, mobile, keyboard, and failure paths?

### Takeaway
The normal desktop route works, bilingual aliases and specialty price explanations are useful, and price-data failure is handled visibly. Mobile modal clipping, unnamed ordinary dialogs, mouse-only SVG controls, inaccessible data context and early D3 failure prevent reliable use as a counter/kitchen reference.

### Inferences
- Replace ordinary dialog with a named native dialog or fully implemented equivalent; preserve deep links and focus behavior, make mobile summary single-column and validate every cut type.
- Use list/search/table as primary operable interface; SVG can be synchronized visual enhancement. This improves low-input/voice use, mobile touch, screen-reader access and offline reference simultaneously.
- A responsive data table beside charts is more reliable than squeezing many11px row labels and10px prices into a320px chart. Distinct point shapes/text keys would also communicate countries without color alone.

### Gaps
- This was bounded local Chromium/DevTools evidence, not production-site testing, screen-reader testing, contrast conformance, Safari/Firefox verification, physical-device testing, 200%/400% zoom, forced-colors, printing or human counter/kitchen field acceptance.
- True cold offline whole-page navigation was not tested; dependency absence and JSON absence were tested in copied fixtures. No source code was changed to run these fixtures.
- A preview server was started only for index/assets/data copied to `/tmp`, and was stopped by its own session31531 after completion. No other server was stopped.

## Which enhancements would make this exceptional, and how should completion be judged?

### Takeaway
First repair evidence and operability; then establish a reviewed bilingual atlas and useful buying/cooking workflows; then add professional costing/market tools with explicit inputs and provenance. Grow beyond beef only after the shared taxonomy and beef journeys are demonstrably trustworthy.

### Inferences
- Consumer flagship journey: name/scan-free lookup→identify region/cut form→compare useful alternatives→price source/date check→buy amount→safe preparation card→save/print. Professional flagship journey: cut/alias→reviewed spec and trim→supplier quote comparison in matched units→usable yield/portion costing→save or print evidence-bearing specification.
- Suggested sequencing: (1) trust and accessibility repairs; (2) canonical reviewed catalog and reader directory; (3) price provenance/history plus consumer buying; (4) measured professional specification/costing; (5) offline/print and carefully reviewed additional species. A modest modular zero-build architecture can support early phases; a rewrite/framework is not a prerequisite.
- The Bauhaus identity can remain; extra copy, sources and controls should support decisions rather than add technical pipeline details to the reader flow.

### Gaps
- No implemented enhancements or approval for external publication are implied by these proposals.
- Professional reference editions, Argentine primary anatomical references, translations, culinary instructions and design accessibility require dedicated follow-on review and human acceptance before claims of exceptional/professional readiness.
