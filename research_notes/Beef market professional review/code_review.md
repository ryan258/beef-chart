# Code architecture, pipeline integrity, operations, and Git review

## What exists today, and what evidence supports its architecture and integrity?

### Takeaway
This is a small, useful, manually refreshed beef-cut educational prototype: a static HTML application with multiple price sources and a snapshot pipeline. It has no historical observation store, business-grade market model, scheduler, backend, source-health ledger, or reproducible publication gate. Its source distinctions and atomic writes are useful foundations, but several documented assurances exceed implementation and current snapshot evidence.

### Inferences
- This is a cut-reference and retail-price research starting point, not yet a physical cattle/beef market research terminal. Retail advertisements, national/GBA average consumer prices, and one online catalog cannot establish live cattle, carcass, boxed beef, export parity, packer margins, or trade values.

- Low graph fanout does not make economically material data transformations low business risk.

- No full-suite run is claimed. Minimal offline probes are documented below. Existing pass/fail history was not treated as present validation.

## What corrections and solution proposals should be prioritized?

### Takeaway
First establish evidence-correct prices, secure serving, and honest fallback/accessibility behavior. Then create a canonical cattle/beef observation model with independently refreshable adapters and retained history; only after that build physical commodity analysis and hedging research. Current unavailable observations and last-valid reference observations must remain separate.

- **P0 evidence layer:** versioned snapshot schema; raw response receipt with source URI, retrieval time, provider publication and effective dates, hash, parser version, status, coverage, geography, terms; positive/finite/unit/type invariants; source status separate from observation status; new reference/current/last-valid channels; explicit FX lag and basis; per-view method/date comparability warnings.
- **P0 usability and access:** data table available without D3; all charts have tabular alternatives; cut diagram and cards keyboard-selectable through accessible controls; consistent modal naming/focus; Spanish/English text and synonyms; no-results/multiple-result selection; kg/lb and native currency choices; saved low-input research workspaces, voice-friendly routes and shareable state. See content reviewer for exact additional findings.
- **P1 canonical domain registry:** stable cattle category and cut IDs; bilingual names/aliases; species and category; muscles/anatomical origin; bovine age/sex category; bone/trim/packing/fresh/chilled/frozen; grade/quality; certification and source standard IDs; exact/partial/regional/culinary relationship with confidence and source, avoiding forced equivalent cuts. Keep source product/SKU identity separate from cut identity and physical market instrument identity.
- **P1 Observation model:** observation_id; series_id; instrument/product_id; market_stage (liveweight cattle, dressed/carcass, primal/boxed, retail, trade); market/location/region; category/grade/spec; transaction/offer/reference/average distinction; native value/currency/unit; weight basis (live, dressed, product); effective_from/to; published_at; retrieved_at; available/as_of/status; sample count/coverage; aggregation method; source_receipt_id; supersedes/revision ID. Derived conversions store exact FX observation ID, convention, rate, native unrounded value, conversion factor, calculation version; never overwrite native data.
- **P1 Adapter architecture:** `adapters/{bls,indec,bcra,usda,carrefour,...}` fetch/parse into shared schema; `validation/` gates; immutable `raw/receipts` and local SQLite/Parquet observations; `derivations/` for FX/unit/calendar joins; `publish/` validated public JSON/CSV and manifest; `web/` modules with cut registry and presentation logic. Adapter-specific source failures do not destroy observation history or misstate freshness.
- **P1 research terminal:** dated market-stage dashboard; synchronized history and revisions; native/converted/real price comparison; category/spec and geographic filters; spread analysis only for declared compatible stage/basis; source-health board; calendar and publication lag; downloadable data with provenance and generated research brief. Default to personal local research, with optional distribution only on Ryan's explicit delegation.
- **P2 physical economics:** model explicitly supplied dressing percentage and saleable yield; purchase live/carcass weight to output cuts; byproduct credits; packer/cutting costs; cold chain/freight, shrink, trim, spoilage, tariffs/tax assumptions, payment/financing and FX date; scenario bounds and dimensional checks. Yield must be supported by specified carcass/sample, not illustrative graphics. Trade parity and margins require stage-compatible source observations.
- **P2 hedging research:** futures instrument/contract specifications and historical settlements licensed/verified through authoritative providers; live cattle/feeder cattle hedge basis and relevant cash category; contract-size/weight/currency mapping, roll conventions, historical basis and scenario sensitivity.
