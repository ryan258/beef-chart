# Content, documentation, frontend functionality, accessibility, and professional workflow review

## What exists today, and how should its claims be interpreted?

### Takeaway
The current application is an attractive, zero-build educational beef-cut explorer plus a static retail-price comparison snapshot. It is a useful foundation for an Argentina/USA cattle and beef intelligence tool, but present content, pricing methods, procurement specifications, accessibility, and evidence controls do not support professional-grade claims yet.

### Cited Findings
- Scope/inventory: one 1,844-line `index.html`, a 27-line densely minified override stylesheet, 10 WebP images, one 402-line price snapshot, README, three explanatory docs, roadmap, two shell scripts, one Python scraper and one Python test module. No frontend package/build, application modules, visible CI workflow, historical store, exports, service worker, multi-language catalog, professional watchlist, dashboards, or notification mechanism appeared in the non-ignored file inventory. The monolith contains 20 Argentine retail muscle-cut definitions, 9 US primal definitions, 4 specialty definitions, 12 price-comparison rows; 6 popular-card cuts are explicitly keyboard-enabled. — [Frontend datasets](/Users/ryanjohnson/Projects/beef-chart/index.html:1133), [price rows](/Users/ryanjohnson/Projects/beef-chart/index.html:1199), [directory documentation](/Users/ryanjohnson/Projects/beef-chart/README.md:54).
- UI flow: editorial hero → dual cow explorer → ground-beef benchmark and quick cut search → static carcass flow → retail-price lollipop → static value treemap → popular cards and additional offal. ## Which corrections are substantiated, and what is the evidence level?

### Takeaway
The highest priority corrections are truthful data status, consistent cut specification, graceful loading without D3, and basic modal/SVG accessibility. Browser smoke reproduced several accessibility and fallback failures; this is a bounded validation, not a WCAG audit.

## What is the comprehensive proposal for an exceptional professional experience?

### Takeaway
Build a bilingual, evidence-first workbench for comparing specified cattle/beef observations, diagnosing market drivers and producing reproducible sourcing/valuation decisions. Preserve the cut explorer as its shared reference layer and expose low-effort professional workflows instead of expanding novelty charts first.

### Cited Findings
- Existing sources cover retail observations only; current price records and UI omit live cattle, feeder markets, dressed cattle, wholesale cutout/primal benchmarks, slaughter/supply, exports/imports and professional decision storage. Those are new capabilities proposed below, grounded in the declared user scope and observed current structure. — [source registry](/Users/ryanjohnson/Projects/beef-chart/README.md:40), [price schema](/Users/ryanjohnson/Projects/beef-chart/data/prices.json:1).
- Existing cut explorer's all-cuts lookup, accent-normalized aliases and paired comparison row definitions provide reusable foundation. Existing source/date/FX blocks and missing-value text are useful, but should become reusable tested components with observation-status semantics. — [cut IDs](/Users/ryanjohnson/Projects/beef-chart/index.html:1174), [search normalization](/Users/ryanjohnson/Projects/beef-chart/index.html:1731), [price blocks](/Users/ryanjohnson/Projects/beef-chart/index.html:1565).

### Inferences
**User workflows proposed**
- Morning market view: saved AR/US cattle/beef watchlist, dates/cadence/latency badges, last known verified values, latest changes, meaningful source failure/release notices, bilingual quick commands and resumed last workspace. Never imply delayed feed is live.
- Cut/spec lookup: speak Spanish/English/common aliases or IPCVA/IMPS code, inspect muscle and trim/bone/fat/membrane description, compare exact vs partial equivalents, open source and save procurement specification; cow is optional navigation.
- Cross-border comparison: choose market level and geography first, then specification, grade/category, basis/date and currency/unit; show comparability warnings, original values, conversions, overlap window, sample size/weighting and freshness. Connecting spread is permitted only when pair passes explicit compatibility rules.
- Procurement/landed-cost worksheet: quantity, supplier quote/spec, FX assumption, freight, insurance, tariffs/taxes, duties, loss/yield and timing; editable versioned scenarios, break-even/sensitivity, statement of excluded costs, exports with as-of snapshot. Actual legal/trade assumptions must be linked and effective-dated; calculator is not a live trade recommendation.
- Carcass valuation/margin: distinguish liveweight/dressedweight/bone-in/boneless/saleable yield and byproducts; input animal class/dressing/fabrication outcomes, attach empirical model or user scenario, avoid double-counting overlapping cuts, reconcile mass balance and value. Replace illustrative104% Sankey and static value treemap with coherent model or clearly marked teaching scenario.
- Market analyst view: historized source-native observations, revisions/vintages, rolling trends and seasonal/basis spreads, retail-wholesale relationships, ARS prices vs FX vs inflation decomposition, supply/trade context and selectable comparable periods.
- Review/report workflow: shareable saved view or PDF/CSV/XLSX output with source URLs, timestamps, methods, cut specifications, FX basis, vintage, caveats and hash/ID. Keyboard/table alternative, data glossary and transparent source health are part of every professional screen.

**Feasible sequence and acceptance**
1. **Foundation/correction stage.** Fix findings1–8 and numeric/failure guards; rewrite claims; preserve immutable current snapshot with qualification; authoritative cut taxonomy and typed equivalence; source registry/schema/status contract, explicit out-of-stock and staleness, as-of dates next to prices. Acceptance: keyboard all cuts/primals via equivalent table; modal named, hidden/inert when closed and focus returns under every close path; zero-D3 boot preserves prices/search; Sankey mass balance100%; no tradeable quote without eligibility/provenance.
2. **Trusted reference/retail workbench stage.** Extract domain datasets, validation/units/FX and locale layer from monolith; bilingual UI, unit/currency selection, data table with sort/filter/search, view persistence and URL-deep links, direct source drilldown, freshness dashboard, print/CSV export. Maintain existing editorial explorer as reference route. Acceptance: same snapshot produces identical values across chart/table/export, all transformations auditable, EN/ES accessible labels and units, no-result/ambiguous-query feedback, partial sources do not crash whole app.
3. **Historical/professional market stage.** Append-only observations and source artifacts, scheduled ingest/health metrics, revised-vintage tracking, official livestock/wholesale/supply/trade connectors selected with market researcher evidence; analyst watchlists, release calendar, comparisons/basis/seasonality with compatible methodology. Acceptance: history no overwrite, date/source/region/measurement stages preserved, revisions visible, time-series gaps distinct from zero and scheduled sync status shown.
4. **Decision-support stage.** User scenarios for yield/carcass value and landed cost, complete inputs, original-source/default documentation, sensitivity analysis, uncertainty bands only where statistically supported; saved plans/reports, XLSX/PDF evidence export and professional/operator pilot. Acceptance: mass/unit/currency conservation and reproducible calculation IDs, hard block/clear qualification for incompatible values, outside expert review of taxonomy and financial/trade assumptions.
5. **Operational excellence stage.** Appropriate CI targeted checks, documented recovery and source outage procedure, pinned dependencies/CSP, deliberate provider rights/license registry, offline last-known snapshots with conspicuous age, security/user-data retention controls only when accounts/cloud storage introduced, manual AT/device validation and pilot feedback. Scale hosting/DB/auth only when user usage requires it.

**Parallel backlog inventory beyond primary defects**
- Content: complete glossary for retail/wholesale/live/dressed, weight bases, grade/yield, animal categories, inflation index vs unit price, advertised vs transacted price, snapshot/publication/observation dates, FX quote regime, country/region; code/native names and translated aliases; source/editor review date per fact; no unverified health claims; photo rights and photo/spec match.
