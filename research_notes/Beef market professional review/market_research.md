# Professional Argentina–US cattle and beef market research requirements

Research checked 2026-10-09, America/Chicago. Scope confirmed by Ryan: livestock and beef markets, cuts, pricing, procurement and trade. A futures layer is optional hedging research; an equities platform and trade execution are outside this scope. Sources below were opened or searched during this review; a successful page read does not establish a working adapter, current feed availability, permission to redistribute, or successful access to every underlying download. No live market price, tariff applicability to a particular shipment, or investment recommendation is asserted.

## 1. What must change in the project's market meaning and professional data contract?

### Takeaway

The project should become an evidence-based physical cattle and beef research workbench. Its useful cut explorer and retail snapshots are a starting point, but cattle, carcasses, wholesale cuts, advertised features, realized retail averages and customs trade unit values require separate identities and comparison rules.

### Cited Findings

- The current README describes live/real-time retail intelligence and names BLS monthly averages, INDEC GBA averages, USDA weekly grocery features and Carrefour catalog prices. The pipeline specification shows monthly statistical prices and monthly/daily FX conversions; these are different sampling populations and time bases, not a unified tradable market price. Local sources: [README](/Users/ryanjohnson/Projects/beef-chart/README.md), [pipeline documentation](/Users/ryanjohnson/Projects/beef-chart/docs/data-pipeline.md).
- The checked snapshot has 17 US records and 19 Argentine records; its overall `fetched` timestamp is 2026-10-07T19:51:30Z, monthly FX period is 2026-08 and `fx.latest.date` is 2026-08-31. A recent retrieval timestamp therefore does not establish a recent underlying observation. [Current snapshot](/Users/ryanjohnson/Projects/beef-chart/data/prices.json).
- USDA Livestock Mandatory Reporting covers livestock purchases and domestic/export boxed-beef sales. Its purpose is market price and supply transparency, with confidentiality constraints. [AMS LMR compliance Q&A](https://www.ams.usda.gov/rules-regulations/mmr/lmr/compliance/FAQ).
- USDA excludes some transactions that would distort general market conditions or levels and publishes excluded-transaction summaries. A benchmark is an explicitly constructed population rather than all cattle or all beef transactions. [AMS excluded transactions](https://www.ams.usda.gov/rules-regulations/mmr/lmr/excluded-transactions).
- ERS price spreads express farm and wholesale values on a retail-weight basis using standard-animal conversion factors. Its documented beef factors are 2.40 pounds live steer and 1.14 pounds wholesale beef per pound retail product. Those are methodology factors, not measured yield promises for a specific animal. [ERS spread methodology](https://www.ers.usda.gov/data-products/meat-price-spreads/documentation).
- USDA IMPS provides procurement specifications and separate fresh beef and variety-meat series; it also offers Spanish versions. Item identity alone is insufficient: its ordering framework covers grade, refrigeration, fat limits, aging, portion dimensions, packaging and assurance. [AMS IMPS catalog](https://www.ams.usda.gov/grades-standards/imps), [published Fresh Beef Series 100](https://www.ams.usda.gov/sites/default/files/media/IMPS_100_Fresh_Beef%5B1%5D.pdf).
- A concrete correction is needed in the cut guide: it calls entraña fina “inside skirt.” IPCVA defines entraña fina as diaphragm; IMPS 121C outside skirt is diaphragm, whereas IMPS 121D inside skirt is transversus abdominis. This supports an anatomical outside-skirt relationship, with preparation qualifiers, rather than the current inside-skirt label. [Local cut guide](/Users/ryanjohnson/Projects/beef-chart/docs/cuts-guide.md), [IPCVA entraña fina](https://ipcva.agrositio.com/nomenclador2015/cortes-detalle.php?categoria_id=6&corte_id=91), [IMPS Series 100, printed p. 41](https://www.ams.usda.gov/sites/default/files/media/IMPS_100_Fresh_Beef%5B1%5D.pdf).
- IPCVA defines vacío as a set of abdominal-wall muscles and fascias, including external/internal obliques, rectus abdominis and transversus abdominis. Treating vacío and a narrowly specified US flank steak as automatically interchangeable is therefore unsafe for procurement. [IPCVA vacío](https://ipcva.agrositio.com/nomenclador2015/cortes-detalle.php?categoria_id=6&corte_id=82).
- Argentina's Resolution 32/2018 approves bovine category and carcass classification frameworks; a further official annex describes meat-quality classification. These are local grading systems, not an automatic translation into USDA Prime/Choice/Select. [Resolution 32/2018](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/normativa/nacional/316048/texto), [official meat-classification annex](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/normativa/330589_res195_pdf/archivo).

### Inferences and solution proposals

These are design proposals derived from the distinctions above, not claims that this functionality exists today.

1. **P0 — Correct product claims.** Replace “live/real-time market intelligence” with the actual source-specific cadence. Show observation period, publication time, retrieval time and most recent successful validation separately. Freshly retrieved old data must visibly remain old.
2. **P0 — Introduce market stages.** Separate live cattle, feeder/finished cattle, dressed carcass, wholesale boxed beef, retail advertised features, retail survey prices and retailer catalog offers. Different stages remain discoverable together but cannot be silently ranked as equivalent.
3. **P0 — Define dimensions before adding feeds.** Each observation should include source/report/series ID, raw description, country/region/location, currency, price unit, quantity unit, weight basis, animal category, grade, cut specification, trim/bone/membrane, fresh/chilled/frozen, domestic/export destination, sale mechanism, delivery window, observed period, release/revision status, sample count/volume where available, raw evidence hash and adapter version.
4. **P0 — Make comparison eligibility explicit.** A comparison should state comparable, qualified approximation, or incompatible. A currency-and-weight conversion never establishes anatomical, grade, geography, sale-channel or tax equivalence. Unknown qualifications remain unknown.
5. **P0 — Preserve raw values.** Store source currency and unit before transformation. USD/lb is a selectable display, not the canonical identity. Professionals also need ARS/kg, USD/kg, USD/cwt, head and metric tons, retaining the denominator's basis.
6. **P0 — Correct entraña semantics in data, UI, dictionary and matching rules together.** Distinguish inside skirt, outside skirt, skin/membrane removed and unspecified skirt. Audit the downstream prices attached to each cut after correcting labels. Preserve bife de costilla, boneless strip and T-bone distinctions.
7. **P1 — Build a bilingual specification catalog.** Represent Argentine nomenclador codes and US IMPS codes as separate authoritative identifiers. Relationships should be exact within a stated specification, partial overlap, adjacent anatomy, culinary substitute, or unknown. A culinary substitution is not procurement equivalence.
8. **P1 — Separate grade, breed, feeding claim and certification.** “Grass-fed,” “Angus,” “Choice,” Argentine carcass grade and export eligibility are independent fields. Do not infer one from a cut name or photograph.
9. **P1 — Add evidence-backed yield scenarios.** Distinguish hot carcass yield, chilled loss, saleable fabrication yield and cooked/edible yield. Require explicit denominator and assumptions; professional calculations should accept measured lot yields and ranges. Label illustrative treemap yields and sensory radar scores as editorial until sourced.
10. **P1 — Give every number an evidence drawer.** Users should see the original report/item, qualifiers, calculation, exclusions, transformation and last refresh outcome without reconstructing the pipeline.
11. **P1 — Use availability states.** Missing, not published, suppressed, no trading, no stock, unsupported cut, parse failure, blocked source, stale and carried forward must be distinct. None is a zero price. Source-specific outage should preserve last good evidence with its original age.
12. **P1 — Keep history and revisions.** An append-only observation archive plus versioned corrections supports charts, seasonality, auditability and “what was known then” analysis. A replacement snapshot alone cannot support credible time-series research.
13. **P1 — Make accessible tables first-class.** Anatomy and charts enrich a sortable, bilingual table with concise basis descriptions. Provide saved views, compact voice-friendly commands, keyboard controls, large targets, reduced motion and recoverable task state.

### Gaps

- This task did not validate every anatomical mapping, USDA item number, Argentine grading annex applicability, product photograph or sensory score. A comprehensive cut/specification review remains necessary.
- Retail sources do not establish cattle/wholesale coverage or lot-specific realized purchasing economics. Actual suppliers, quote types, quality tolerances and preferred geographies must be supplied by prospective users or observed in bounded discovery.
- Government methodology factors do not determine the yield, quality or margin of Ryan's own procurement lot. Measured or explicitly assumed lot information remains required.

### Inferences and solution proposals

1. **Source registry:** store owning institution, source URL/report ID, documentation, method/version, units, geographic scope, cadence, expected publication window, actual observation coverage, status/quality flags, terms/rights, attribution, approved use (local analysis/display/export), authentication, quota, timezone, last success/failure and replacement source. “Rights unknown” is an explicit state.
2. **Adapter priority:** AMS physical cattle + boxed beef, MAG/SIO cattle, direct BCRA and validated cut catalog first; ERS/NASS/Argentina fundamentals and INDEC trade second; licensed futures last. Keep existing retail features as demand-context panels.
3. **Adapter acceptance:** retain an actual raw response/report; map all qualifiers; reconcile a sample to the official publication; prove missing/suppressed/revised handling; reproduce formulas; simulate source failure without erasing history; declare limits/rights. A URL listed in README is not an accepted adapter.
4. **Structured transport first:** prefer verified official CSV/JSON/XLSX APIs/downloads over PDF or catalog scraping. Keep a manual evidence-backed import route for blocked or non-machine-readable sources. Do not silently replace an inaccessible benchmark with a convenient retailer price.
5. **Historical rigor:** keep separate publication and observation dates, release-vintage snapshots and source revisions. Align weekly/monthly series deliberately; nearest-date joins should be inspectable. Reject joins with no available FX observation on or before the intended as-of date.
6. **Operational economy:** schedule by actual cadence, cache unchanged reports, perform conditional retrieval where supported, bound retries and record failures. No repeated expensive full analysis is needed to know that a monthly source is unchanged.
7. **Sampling:** retain counts, weights and aggregation definition. Store-weighted advertisements, SKU medians, transaction-volume weighted prices and official statistical averages cannot share a generic “average price” label.
8. **Feed rights:** separate original code, government data, retailer data, exchange data, images and derivative calculations in documentation. Local research authorization does not establish publication, data licensing or paid-feed subscription approval.

## 3. Which professional workflows, calculations and acceptance gates create exceptional utility?

### Takeaway

The strongest differentiator is a defensible bridge between anatomy, market evidence and a saved procurement/trade scenario. Prioritize reproducible comparisons and yield/landed-cost analysis before adding quote density or forecasting.

### Cited Findings

- SENASA explains that country-level market opening is only the first step; individual establishments must also meet destination requirements. [SENASA destination authorization](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/senasa/exportaciones-importaciones-y-estadisticas/exportaciones-e-importaciones/habilitacion-de-destino-de-exportacion/habilitacion-de-destino-de-exportacion-productos-de-origen-animal).
- FSIS import guidance requires eligible country/product/establishment status and identifies APHIS animal-disease restrictions, US labeling and import reinspection requirements. Food safety eligibility, animal-health conditions and customs entry are separate checks. [FSIS import guidance](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/inspection/import-export/import-guidance).
- Current USITC HTS search results contain a 2026 Argentina lean-beef-trimmings provision with specified tariff codes, quantitative limit and dates; a CBP March 26, 2026 message separately describes a second-quarter tranche. This establishes the need for product/date/entry-specific rules, not the eligibility of a proposed shipment or present remaining quota. [USITC HTS search](https://hts.usitc.gov/search?query=02013), [CBP dated tranche notice](https://content.govdelivery.com/accounts/USDHSCBP/bulletins/4102a02?reqfrom=share).
- ERS international-trade documentation identifies the underlying Census trade statistics and commodity aggregation/conversion methodology. Trade volume and customs values are useful evidence but require careful treatment of classifications and weight equivalence. [ERS trade documentation](https://www.ers.usda.gov/data-products/livestock-and-meat-international-trade-data/documentation).
- BCRA's reference and nominal monthly average are identifiable official series, while BYMA defines different implicit parities for MEP and CCL settlement. A single “the dollar” assumption cannot describe every analytical or commercial conversion. [BCRA series](https://www.bcra.gob.ar/tipo-cambio-referencia-comunicacion-a-3500-tcnpm/), [BYMA index definitions](https://www.byma.com.ar/en/newsroom/byma-introduces-two-new-indexes-for-the-argentine-market).

### Inferences and solution proposals

#### P0: defensible calculations and information design

1. **Unit engine:** treat 1 international avoirdupois pound as 0.45359237 kg; US cwt as 100 lb; metric tonne as 1,000 kg. Encode definitions rather than rounded chained conversions. Keep US hundredweight explicit to avoid confusion with other hundredweights. These are arithmetic unit definitions; displayed rounding belongs at the end.
2. **Currency engine:** for `r` ARS/USD, an ARS/kg price `p` converts to USD/kg `p/r` and USD/lb `(p/r)*0.45359237`. USD/cwt converts to USD/lb by dividing by 100; US cents/lb converts by dividing by 100. Distinguish price denomination from amount settlement currency.
3. **Weight-basis engine:** liveweight-to-carcass or carcass-to-saleable conversions require yield assumptions; USD/lb unit conversion alone never performs them. Expose yields separately and display source and uncertainty. Reject undocumented live/carcass/product/CWE/retail comparisons.
4. **FX policy:** preserve reference rate, rate date, bid/ask or average type, series identity, as-of cutoff and month-aggregation rule. Display official and financial FX as separate scenarios where evidence permits. A monthly-price/monthly-average-FX quotient is an analytical approximation; it is not necessarily a monthly mean of daily dollar prices. Do not bake a historic export settlement blend into the app.
5. **Basis banner:** every comparison states market stage, geography, period, freshness, bone/trim, quality, sale channel and FX convention. Comparability problems should be visible beside the calculation.
6. **Evidence-preserving downloads:** CSV/JSON exports include native values, normalized values, calculation versions and source links. Exchange datasets remain export-gated by documented permissions. Export is an artifact, not permission to redistribute upstream data.

#### P1: daily professional research and procurement workbench

7. **Saved market brief:** livestock prices, wholesale cut benchmarks, FX, production/faena, inventories/cold storage, trade flows and upcoming releases, showing what changed since the user's last visit. One voice-friendly saved command should reopen the exact view and comparison.
8. **Compare lot/quote:** select product specification, region, grade, fresh/frozen, delivery date and quantity; compare imported/manual supplier quotes against an eligible benchmark with transport, tax, credit/payment and minimum-order differences stated. Unsupported attributes stay unknown.
9. **Supplier quote archive:** track quote date, expiration, quoted currency, product specification, volume, packing, supplier location, delivery terms, payment terms, validity and evidence attachment. Separate offers, purchases, receipts and invoices; a catalog offer is not a completed transaction.
10. **Saleable cost calculator:** input purchased weight `W`, price `P`, yield `y` and applicable costs/credits; compute saleable quantity `W*y` and effective cost `(W*P + additional_costs - byproduct_credits)/(W*y)`. Require `0<y<=1`, consistent units and currency timestamps. Give separate buy/fabricate/sell assumptions rather than an unexplained margin score.
11. **Carcass balance:** account for all saleable cuts, offal, trim, bones, fat, losses and waste against a defined carcass denominator. Make mass balance and revenue allocation visible. Edible/non-edible offal must remain separate; retailer “molleja” ambiguity cannot supply assured gland identity.
12. **Cut substitution:** show anatomical overlap, yield effect, grade/trim differences, processing changes and customer acceptance requirements. A cheap substitute has to satisfy the stated specification; no algorithm should declare it equivalent solely on a translated name.
13. **Market metrics:** day/week/month/year change; grade spread such as Choice–Select with same basis/period; region spread; wholesale item relative to composite; live/dressed relation with explicit assumptions; head counts, slaughter weights, production/faena, stock/cold storage, female slaughter share when sourced, import/export quantity and value, partner concentration and seasonality.
14. **Meaningful spread labels:** farm-to-retail/wholesale spreads are context, not business profit. A local quote–benchmark spread requires aligned geography/grade/unit/date. A retailer SKU median difference across countries is a descriptive difference, not an arbitrage signal.
15. **Real-price views:** optional CPI-deflated domestic trends with named index, geography and base month. Nominal ARS, inflation-adjusted ARS, official-USD and financial-USD trends answer different questions and require separate labels.
16. **Trade flow research:** separate chilled/frozen, boneless/bone-in, edible offal, live cattle and prepared products using versioned commodity codes. Compute customs unit value `value/quantity` only for compatible product weight; label it a shipment-composition average rather than a quoted cut price. Never divide a product-value series by CWE quantities without deliberately defined interpretation.
17. **Coverage and confidence view:** display reporting volume/count, source-method limits, suppressed cells, unobserved attributes and comparison eligibility. Show actual evidence limits; avoid a precise confidence percentage without a defensible statistical model.
18. **Change alerts:** user-set conditions for prices, FX, missing data, release updates and changed eligibility/source terms. Notification should explain the exact evidence change and preserve original/revised records. Keep quiet when unchanged.

#### P1: cross-border trade scenarios

19. **Versioned rulebook:** jurisdiction, product code, rule source, effective-from/to, status, establishment and product eligibility, certification, tariff/quota conditions and date last checked. Keep law, administrative bulletin, market-opening announcement and statistical data distinct.
20. **Landed-cost scenario:** choose route and commercial delivery terms; enter product cost, inland freight, ocean/air freight, insurance, handling, cold storage, customs duties, inspection, financing, wastage and FX dates. Calculate costs in a common settlement currency with units reconciled. Avoid double-counting freight already included in the quote's delivery term.
21. **Procurement feasibility states:** cost estimate can be complete while regulatory eligibility or quote validity is unknown. Show each prerequisite and its evidence independently. A cost advantage does not establish legal, sanitary or operational shipment feasibility.
22. **Sensitivity and break-even:** vary FX, purchased price, yield, freight, holding days and losses; report effective cost and explicit break-even values. Persist assumptions with the source vintage. Use ranges without presenting them as predictions.
23. **Rule-refresh discipline:** expiry and stale checks for establishment lists, tariffs, quotas, disease restrictions and destination certificates. Provide authoritative links and bounded human verification steps before any real shipment decision; never treat a historical announcement as present blanket authorization.

#### P2: distinct futures/hedging research layer

24. **Instrument identity:** exchange, product, contract month/year, symbol, quote currency/unit, multiplier, settlement type, expiry/delivery dates, time zone, trading calendar, source timestamp, delay, price type and licensed use. Last trade, bid/ask, settlement and cash index are independent observations.
25. **Curve and cash basis:** compare eligible local cash cattle evidence against a named CME contract, with basis defined explicitly as cash minus futures after unit alignment. Cross-country basis embeds country/FX/quality/logistics differences and is not automatically a hedgeable US location basis.
26. **Continuous-contract discipline:** retain actual contracts; any continuous chart must disclose roll dates, adjustment method and its unsuitable use as an executable quote. Backtests require only information available at that date.
27. **Exposure scenario:** user enters expected eligible weight/time horizon and selects an instrument; illustrate multiplier and residual volume/basis mismatch. Futures price changes, physical operating economics, FX and financing remain separate components. No automated hedge recommendation or broker connection is required for this project scope.
