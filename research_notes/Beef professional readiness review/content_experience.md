## What works now, and which defects or experience gaps are confirmed?

### Takeaway

### Inferences

- Professional acceptance example: choose two actual qualified offers → check specification differences → enter/confirm measured or assumed yield → compare usable cost and portions → export/save the decision with source terms and assumptions → reopen without retyping. Avoid a new framework, account system or data-feed expansion before a concrete task requires it.
