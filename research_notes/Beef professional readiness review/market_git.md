## Which external facts should shape a professional and educator tool?

### Takeaway

A professional workbench needs typed product specifications and source populations before more charts. Educators need the same evidence layer plus explicit models, worked examples, source criticism and stable lesson snapshots. Public official sources support a practical physical-beef-market scope without requiring brokerage or broad investing features.

### Cited Findings

- USDA IMPS are voluntary consensus purchasing specifications, used by foodservice and institutional buyers. The official index separates current documents from drafts and offers Fresh Beef Series 100 and Variety Meats Series 700; Spanish fresh-beef documents also exist. Pin the exact document/edition/item rather than adopting a draft or translated common name as a universal specification. [USDA IMPS index](https://www.ams.usda.gov/grades-standards/imps).
- AMS publishes distinct daily/weekly cattle and beef reports, including boxed-beef cutout/cuts, trimmings, variety meats and byproduct values. The present app's grocery-feature observations are a separate retail population. [AMS daily beef directory](https://www.ams.usda.gov/market-news/daily-beef-reports), [weekly/monthly directory](https://www.ams.usda.gov/market-news/weekly-and-monthly-beef-reports).
- The January 2026 cutout guide defines cutout as estimated gross carcass value. Daily reports separate Choice/Select, 0–21-day domestic negotiated delivery, FOB plant and USD/cwt. The AM/PM report windows differ and PM is cumulative. Cutout excludes fabrication costs and hide/offal revenue; its derivation uses item prices, yields and weighted relationships. A cutout-retail spread cannot be presented as a processor profit calculation. [AMS cutout guide, January 2026](https://www.ams.usda.gov/sites/default/files/media/LMRBoxedBeefCutoutsUserGuide.pdf).
- Livestock Mandatory Reporting is intended to improve comparable market information for slaughter cattle and meat markets. This is authoritative market context, but only for the population/transaction definitions each report covers. [AMS LMR](https://www.ams.usda.gov/rules-regulations/mmr/lmr).
- MyMarketNews publishes raw report data through an API. Its basic guide requires registration/personal API key and states it does not support open web-browser calls. A new adapter therefore needs secure local/server-side credentials, or a public-file/manual-import path, and a report-specific capability check. Do not embed a key in static JavaScript or promise keyless access based on current public PDF ingestion. [MMN basic instructions](https://mymarketnews.ams.usda.gov/mars-api/getting-started/basic-instructions), [API introduction](https://mymarketnews.ams.usda.gov/mymarketnews-api).
- Grocery feature reports collect advertised consumer prices, primarily from public retailer websites; feature activity is distinct from purchases or national transaction averages. Feature rate, outlet counts, activity index and grade labels have defined meanings. [AMS grocery-feature directory](https://www.ams.usda.gov/market-news/grocerystore), [retail terms](https://www.ams.usda.gov/market-news/livestock-poultry-and-grain-retail-terms).
- BLS food average prices standardize unit prices and exclude sales tax. BLS warns that average-price changes can reflect sample rotation, quality and consumer-preference changes, and missing publications can follow sample/availability limits; pure price inflation is the CPI's separate purpose. [BLS average-price methodology](https://www.bls.gov/cpi/factsheets/average-prices.htm).
- INDEC's current CPI portal distinguishes national/regional indexes and average prices by geography; its average-price historical file includes regional and GBA coverage. This supports explicit GBA retail context and separate inflation analysis, not a nationwide Argentine wholesale-price claim. [INDEC CPI/average-price portal](https://www.indec.gob.ar/Nivel4/Tema/3/5/31).
- BCRA provides the A3500 wholesale reference and nominal monthly-average exchange-rate history in a downloadable series, and an official API directory. “Official dollar” alone is an inadequate conversion specification; retain exact series, convention, date, retrieval and why that rate fits the calculation. [BCRA A3500/TCNPM history](https://www.bcra.gob.ar/tipo-cambio-referencia-comunicacion-a-3500-tcnpm/), [BCRA APIs](https://www.bcra.gob.ar/apis-banco-central/).
- Argentina's government livestock portal links official bovine statistics, slaughter-information systems and SIO Carnes. SIO's public site is reachable, but this review did not accept a feed/schema/license/coverage or reconcile a transaction. Government linkage supports evaluating these candidates, not claiming integration or access. [Argentina Ganadería](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/economia/agricultura/ganaderia), [SIO Carnes](https://siocarnes.magyp.gob.ar/).
- ERS international livestock/meat trade combines official Census data with commodity conversions. Meat is offered on a carcass-weight-equivalent basis; live animal numbers stay in heads. The current page says long-format machine-readable CSV accompanies the Excel tables after the September 2026 format update. Preserve CWE/product-weight/head distinctions and pin release/schema vintages. [ERS livestock/meat trade](https://www.ers.usda.gov/data-products/livestock-and-meat-international-trade-data).
- USDA explicitly says most site information is public domain but some material has copyright/trademark/patent/personal-use restrictions; attribution is requested and linked sites carry their own terms. Every dataset, photograph, diagram, logo and third-party reference therefore needs its own provenance/use record before shareable educational packs. [USDA digital rights policy](https://www.usda.gov/about-usda/policies-and-links).
- Official beef materials distinguish grading/standards, certified programs and purchasing specifications; a heuristic radar does not establish any one of them. [AMS beef standards](https://www.ams.usda.gov/grades-standards/beef), [carcass-grade standard](https://www.ams.usda.gov/sites/default/files/media/CarcassBeefStandard.pdf).

### Inferences

Recommended phases:

1. **Accepted reference and evidence:** resolve current concrete defects from the other reviews, canonical catalog/crosswalk, explicit source semantics, units, provenance-rich exports, saved state, minimal reproducible setup and rights register. Exit: one cut/spec/observation reconciles everywhere and reopens with minimal input.
2. **Useful teaching and physical-market context:** one accepted US cattle/boxed-beef source, one scoped Argentine source, immutable history/receipts, source-literacy lessons and frozen examples. Exit: one bilingual lesson and one market brief reproduce their evidence without live-provider dependence.
3. **Buyer work:** private offers, measured/assumed yield, usable cost and saved reviewable scenarios. Exit: a buyer compares two real qualified offers and independently reproduces the decision.
4. **Trade and scale only where useful:** trade/fundamentals, landed-cost/eligibility, deeper economics and optional licensed hedging. Exit: source rights, rule versions, dimensional calculations and unresolved feasibility are inspectable in a real scoped task.

Start with a small accepted basket, not all countries/cuts/species/feeds. Keep the distinctive atlas, share one canonical evidence model across reference/market/teaching/calculation views, and extract small modules as needed. A rewrite, authentication platform, paid feed or mobile app is not a prerequisite for the first exceptional workflow.
