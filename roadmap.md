The October 12 [comprehensive review](reports/Beef%20chart%20comprehensive%20review.md) defines the broader direction. The current implementation is an educational atlas and stored retail-context reference, with low-input exploration. Professional workflows below remain future work.

## Current visual state — October 12, 2026

Implemented: all 24 catalog cuts have transparent vintage halftone illustrations; six popular cards, four specialty cards, and cut dossiers use the set. Ribeye and sirloin are distinct. The cow hero and pasture use matching halftone art, and SVG/PNG cow favicons are installed. Interactive cut-paper diagrams retain 20 Argentine cut controls and nine U.S. region controls, with full-name hover/focus readouts, keyboard activation, and hatched layer references. Maps stack through 800px, and narrow dossiers reserve room for the close control.

The [artwork record](docs/artwork-prompts.md) contains retained prompts, asset mappings, sources, and desktop/mobile browser evidence. These visual updates preserve cut identities, price mappings, chart colors, and teaching shares. Source rights and specialist anatomical acceptance are still open.

Deterministic/local browser checks are not professional, production, real-provider, device/AT, or WCAG acceptance. Catalog pagination and delivery-region coverage are not implemented. Source/image rights and specialist crosswalk acceptance remain open. Price values and original observation/FX dates were not refreshed during these changes.

## Phase 2 — Exceptional enthusiast reference

Implemented: bilingual operation (English / Castellano porteño) via `assets/i18n.js` and in-place header toggle, with Rioplatense voseo, URL query parameter (`?lang=es-AR` / `?lang=en`) precedence over optional `localStorage` preference, full-page dynamic translation across all 24 cut dossiers, diagrams, tables, and search feedback, bilingual accent-insensitive search with Argentine aliases, and preserved price evidence, numeric units, and organ withholding invariants.

Future work: canonical reviewed catalog expansion; explained substitutes/comparison, butcher cards, product-aware preparation/safety references, editable quantities, and saved task state. Exit: a user chooses an unfamiliar cut, makes an understandable purchase request, reaches sourced preparation guidance, and resumes with minimal re-entry.

## Phase 3 — Practical professional workbench

Reviewed product specifications, private manual supplier quotes, actual or explicitly assumed fabrication yield, usable/portion costing, and reproducible saved/exported scenarios. Exit: a professional compares two real qualified offers and independently reproduces their usable-cost decision.

## Phase 4 — Dependable market context

Independent accepted adapters, original receipts, immutable observations/revisions, a chosen U.S./Argentine professional source, and reviewed current FX transport/convention. Exit: each adopted series reconciles to retained native source evidence and survives missing/revised/error states without false comparability.

## Phase 5 — Reviewed breadth and use

Selected nutrition, training, offline/print, imports, and meaningful change features, followed by chosen regions/species or landed-cost needs. Expansion follows actual task value and domain acceptance. A planned feature is not shipped evidence; future scheduling, provider access, commits, pushing and publication remain separate owner-controlled actions.
